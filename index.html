Personal UX projects

A dictionary that reads the sentence, not just the word.

A dictionary lists every meaning of a word and leaves the reader to work out which one fits. I built a Chrome extension that reads the sentence around the word and gives the one meaning that fits: on a security page, "salting" is about passwords, not food. It works on email shorthand and Hinglish too.

One less thing to figure out, in the middle of reading.

Plan + Explore for Rapido: a day out in one app.

A visitor with one free day in a city plans it across four apps: ideas in one, reviews in another, routes in a map, then a ride booked four times. I designed one flow inside Rapido to find places, check the route works, and book every ride between stops. A concept, designed solo over six weeks.
